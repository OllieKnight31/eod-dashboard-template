Logins

Who can sign in, and what they see. You are the owner: the only login that can open Settings. Give everyone else their own login below.

Manager: sees Reports, the Scorecard for everyone and the Client journey, and can file any report. No access to Settings.

Setter, Triage, Closer: sees only Submit EOD, with only their own seat, and their own scorecard. Their name is filled in for them.

Add a login

Send them this password yourself. They can change it after signing in, and you can reset it here at any time.

Team

Who sits in each seat. These names fill the pickers on the report, are who the reminder chases, and are who the scorecard expects a report from.

Discord user ID makes a reminder actually notify the person: in Discord turn on User Settings → Advanced → Developer Mode, then right-click them → Copy User ID.

Also known as are other spellings that should file as the same person (Jay, Jamie S). Untick Remind for someone who is on the list but never chased.

Daily targets

Per person, per day. These drive On target / Below / Critical on the scorecard and the dots in the digest. The team tiles multiply them up: target × people × days. 0 switches a target off.

Reminders & daily digest

The reminder names whoever has not filed, in their own EOD channel. The digest sums up the team’s day in daily-standup. The reminder days are also the days the scorecard expects a report.

My password

Change the password you sign in with. If you ever lose it, the setup code on the sign-in page resets the owner login.
